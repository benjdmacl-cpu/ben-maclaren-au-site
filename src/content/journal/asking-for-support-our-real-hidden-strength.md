You've probably heard it takes a village to raise a child. The same goes for adults of all ages. Everyone needs support — it's one of our greatest strengths as humans to both ask for help and to provide it.

Somewhere along the way, a lot of us picked up the idea that needing help is a sign of weakness, something to push through alone before admitting defeat. It's the opposite. Recognising you need support, and doing something about it, takes more self-awareness than pretending you don't.

Seeking counselling is completely normal. Even counsellors see counsellors — it's part of the job, not an exception to it.
