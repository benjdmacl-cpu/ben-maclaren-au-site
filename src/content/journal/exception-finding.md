Problems rarely happen 100% of the time, even when they feel constant. There are usually exceptions — moments when the anxiety was a bit quieter, the argument didn't happen, the bad day didn't fully take hold — that get overlooked because they're not the part that stands out.

Exception-finding is just the deliberate work of noticing those moments and asking what was actually different about them. Who were you with? What had you done differently that day? What were you telling yourself?

Those details usually point at something you're already doing, at least sometimes, that's worth doing more of — which is a much faster starting point than inventing a solution from nothing.
