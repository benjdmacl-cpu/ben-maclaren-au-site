One of the questions I'll sometimes ask early on: "Suppose you went to sleep tonight, and while you were asleep a miracle happened and the problem that brought you here was solved. When you woke up, how would you know? What would be different?"

It sounds like a strange question the first time you hear it, but it does real work. Most people arrive very clear on what's wrong and much less clear on what "better" would actually look like day to day. The miracle question skips past the problem for a moment and asks you to describe the destination instead — concretely, in the small details of an ordinary morning.

Once we know what you're walking toward, it's a lot easier to notice the pieces of it that are already happening, even in small ways, and to build on those rather than starting from scratch.
